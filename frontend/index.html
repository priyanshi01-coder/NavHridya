How it works

Five steps between a recorded call and a ranked queue

Nothing in this chain is a black box. Each stage writes down what it found, and the final number is arithmetic a counsellor can check by hand.

01

Upload with consent

The counsellor confirms the caller was told what the recording is used for. Without that tick, nothing is analysed and NHAA continues exactly as it does today.

02

Transcribe the call

Speech to text handles Hindi, English and the code-mixed speech real callers use. A typed transcript works too, for chat and portal intake.

03

Run the safety rules

Seven explicit rule families - threat to life, weapons, self-harm, imminent danger, sexual violence, violence in progress, child at risk - are matched first, outside the model.

04

Score, explain, triage

Distress and danger are scored with written reasons, combined into the SVI, and turned into a band plus up to four suggested actions for a human to approve.

Key differentiators

What makes this different from a sentiment score

A helpline cannot act on a number it does not understand, and it cannot be put at risk by a model that sounds confident. These six choices follow from that.

Safety rules outside the model

Explicit-risk patterns are evaluated independently and applied afterwards, so a calm voice or a polite phrasing cannot talk a danger flag down. The floor is deterministic and reproducible.

A score with its reasons attached

Every case carries the cues that raised distress, the cues that raised danger, and a line-by-line trail of the arithmetic. A counsellor reviews evidence, not a verdict.

Two axes, not one mood

Distress and danger are scored separately. A grieving caller in no immediate danger and a composed caller with a weapon at the door look nothing alike, and are triaged differently.

Built for the atrocity context

The cue set covers social boycott, untouchability practices, denial of water and shops, pressure to withdraw an FIR, and witness intimidation - the patterns the SC/ST Act exists to address, not generic distress words.

A fixed action list

The model may only choose from six pre-approved recommendations. It cannot invent an instruction, promise a service the helpline does not run, or give clinical advice.

The counsellor closes the case

NAVHRIDYA orders the queue and never closes it. Status changes, escalation and every decision that touches a victim stay with a trained human being.

SVI scoring

The Stress Vulnerability Index is arithmetic, not a guess

The model supplies the two component scores and the indicator flags. Turning those into a number happens in plain Python, which is why the same case always scores the same.

# distress and danger weigh equally
base_score      = 0.5 * distress + 0.5 * danger

# up to four flags, five points each
indicator_bonus = min(20, 5 * flags_true)

svi_score       = min(100, base_score + indicator_bonus)

# a rule fired outside the model: force the floor
if safety_rule_fired:
    svi_score   = max(svi_score, 80)

Bands

  • Low - information and support 0 - 39
  • Moderate - counselling offered 40 - 59
  • High - priority in the queue 60 - 79
  • Critical - escalate now 80 - 100

The weights and the 40/60/80 thresholds are proposed design choices for this prototype. They are not clinically validated, and they are written to be tuned by the helpline rather than hidden inside a model.

Safety by design

What this system deliberately does not do

The people calling 14566 are already carrying enough. A triage layer that guesses wrong in the wrong direction costs more than one that admits its limits.

It does not diagnose

No condition, no clinical label, no therapy. The output is a triage signal for a trained counsellor, and the interface says so on every case.

It does not act on its own

No case is closed, escalated or dismissed automatically. The queue order changes; the decisions do not move.

It does not analyse without consent

Upload is gated on an explicit confirmation that the caller was told what the recording is used for. Recordings stay on the helpline's own machine.

This prototype is not an emergency service

NAVHRIDYA is a Smart India Hackathon 2026 prototype for PS SIH26093, built by Team HackSmiths. It is not an official Government of India service. If someone is in immediate danger, call 112. For atrocity-related help, call NHAA on 14566.
